1. Privacy Policy

Hodler LLC ("Hodler", "Hodler Store", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, purchase our clothing or use our AI-powered fashion features ("Hodler AI"), as well as our shipping, returns and exchange policies.

By using our website and services, you agree to the practices described in these policies.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number and shipping address.
  • Messages, inquiries, styling preferences and feedback you send to us.
  • Order details, including the products, sizes and colors you request.
  • Photos, body measurements or avatar data you choose to provide for AI virtual try-on.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, language and general location.
  • Pages visited, links clicked and time spent on our website.
  • Information from cookies and similar technologies (see Section 6).

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To process, fulfill and deliver orders and arrange exchanges or returns.
  • To provide virtual try-on previews, fit analysis and size recommendations.
  • To personalize styling suggestions and improve our collections.
  • To send newsletters and updates when you have opted in — you may unsubscribe at any time.
  • To maintain the security of our website and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

4. AI Try-On & Body Data

Hodler AI allows you to virtually try on clothing using a photo, a body scan or an avatar. Because this information is personal, we treat it with extra care:

  • Purpose limitation: photos and measurements are used only to generate your try-on previews, fit analysis and size recommendations.
  • No sale of images: we never sell, rent or publish your photos or body data.
  • No facial recognition: we do not use your images to identify you or build biometric identifiers.
  • Retention: uploaded photos are kept only as long as needed to provide the service, or until you delete them or close your profile.
  • Model training: we will not use your personal photos to train AI models without your separate, explicit consent.
  • Deletion on request: you can ask us to delete your try-on data at any time by emailing nambabi2609@gmail.com.

AI-generated previews are visual approximations. Actual fit, color and drape may vary slightly due to lighting, screen settings and fabric behavior.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate our business (for example hosting, email, payment and shipping carriers), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Technology partners that process data on our behalf to provide Hodler AI features, under strict data-protection agreements.
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Hodler, our customers or others.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make our website work and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use it. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some features of the site.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under Washington State law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, including AI try-on data.
  • Withdraw consent and opt out of marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email nambabi2609@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our website and Hodler AI features are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Users under 18 should use our services with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Shipping

  • Orders are prepared and shipped from Washington State, United States.
  • Processing usually takes 1–3 business days after your order is confirmed.
  • Standard delivery within the contiguous United States typically takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.
  • You will receive tracking information by email once your order ships.
  • Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by carriers, weather or peak seasons.
  • For international shipping requests, please contact us before ordering.

11. Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you wear. If something is not right, we are here to help.

  • Contact us within 30 days of delivery to request a return or exchange.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all tags attached.
  • Intimates, swimwear, final-sale and personalized items cannot be returned for hygiene or custom-made reasons, unless defective.
  • Exchanges for a different size or color are subject to availability.
  • Once your return is received and inspected, approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
  • If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, email us with photos within 7 days and we will make it right at no cost to you.

To start a return or exchange, email nambabi2609@gmail.com with your name, order details and reason.

12. Policy Changes

We may update these policies from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated on our website or by email.
