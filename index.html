Our Story

Clothing you keep, not clothing you replace.

Hodler was born in Maple Valley, Washington, from a simple idea: a wardrobe should feel like something you hold on to. Our name is a nod to that belief — we design pieces worth holding.

Every Hodler collection begins with the essentials people actually reach for: a coat that works from Monday commute to Sunday brunch, a knit that softens with every wear, linen that breathes through the warmest days. We focus on clean silhouettes, honest fabrics and colors that pair effortlessly with what is already in your closet.

At the same time, we believe shopping for clothes should be easier. That is why we are building Hodler AI — technology that lets you design, visualize and virtually try on outfits, so you can choose with confidence and spend less time in fitting rooms.

Hodler AI — Fashion Technology

Design, build and try on clothes with artificial intelligence.

Hodler AI is our second pillar: a fashion-technology platform that brings artificial intelligence, 3D garment simulation and virtual reality into the way clothes are designed, made and chosen. See exactly how a piece looks and fits on you — without stepping into a fitting room.

  • Virtual try-on from a single photo or a quick body scan
  • AI-assisted design that turns ideas and sketches into 3D garments
  • Accurate fit and size prediction tailored to your body shape
  • Immersive AR & VR experiences — the fitting room of the future

What Hodler AI Does

One platform, from first sketch to final fit.

👗

AI Virtual Try-On

Upload a photo or create your avatar, choose any garment, and our AI renders it on your body with realistic drape, folds, lighting and proportions — in seconds.

✏️

AI Design Studio

Describe an idea or upload a sketch. Generative AI proposes silhouettes, colorways, prints and fabric combinations, helping designers explore hundreds of options in minutes.

🧵

3D Garment Construction

Every design is built as a true 3D garment from digital sewing patterns, with physics-based fabric simulation so you can see how cotton, wool or silk really moves.

📏

Smart Fit & Size Prediction

Our models analyze body measurements and garment specs to recommend the right size and highlight how each area will fit — shoulders, waist, hips and length.

🥽

AR & VR Fitting Rooms

Step into immersive virtual showrooms, or use augmented reality on your phone and smart mirrors to see clothes on you in real time, from every angle.

✨

Personal AI Stylist

Get complete outfit suggestions based on your style, occasion, weather and the pieces you already own — a stylist that learns what you love.

How Virtual Try-On Works

Your fitting room, anywhere.

Tap a garment on the left to preview the experience. In the full Hodler AI app, every look is rendered on your own body.

01

Create your body profile

Upload one full-length photo or complete a quick phone scan. AI estimates your measurements and builds a private 3D avatar.

02

Choose any garment

Browse the Hodler collection or upload a design. Each piece is stored as a true 3D garment with real fabric properties.

03

See it on you — instantly

Our AI fits the garment to your body, simulating drape, stretch and movement. Rotate, walk and compare looks side by side.

04

Choose with confidence

Get your recommended size, styling suggestions and matching pieces — then decide, knowing exactly how it will look.
